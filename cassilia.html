Character Profile

ONE WOMAN.
COUNTLESS LIVES.

Cassilia is an original character created by TownGG Mod Studio and the central figure connecting several of my Starfield mods.

Before becoming Starborn, she served as a soldier and joined Constellation as an explorer. After crossing the Unity again and again, every new universe gave her another life—and another identity.

The Sword She Should Never Have Stolen

TEMERIUS

Temerius did not originally belong to Cassilia. Before the events of THE TERMINUS, it was held by a dangerous Starborn known as Kadovar, the Oathbreaker.

Cassilia stole the relic from him anyway. Perhaps she believed it was too dangerous to remain in his hands. Perhaps she wanted to learn where it came from. Or perhaps, after surviving so many universes, she believed her will was strong enough to control it.

SHE WAS WRONG.

You can control me. You are stronger than the others. Take what belongs to you. I chose you.

SHE DID NOT DIE.

At the exact moment the player defeated her, Cassilia entered the Unity.

She disappeared from the player's universe and awakened in another one. Her body was not destroyed. Her soul did not remain behind as a ghost.

A Connection Between Universes

CASSILIA'S SOUL

Despite the name, Cassilia is not dead. She is not a ghost, a memory, or a fragment of a lost Starborn. She is still alive and physically exists in another universe.

The energy of the Unity, the influence of Temerius, and the moment of their battle formed a link between Cassilia and the player. Through that soul connection, the player can temporarily pull her across the boundary between universes.

She fights beside the player. When the connection closes, she returns home.

Cassilia and the Player

ENEMIES.
ALLIES.
CONNECTED.

The first time they met, they tried to kill each other. The player took Temerius. Cassilia crossed the Unity and awakened in another universe.

Yet the battle also broke the sword's control and saved her from losing herself completely. Now, separated by an entire universe, they can temporarily fight together.

Cassilia warns the player not to trust Temerius. Silence does not mean the sword has been defeated.

OLD CONSTELLATION UNIFORM

Long before the events of THE TERMINUS, Cassilia was once a member of Constellation. The uniform represents a quieter part of her life: a home, a team, and a place where she belonged.

Another Life She Lived

THE MANTIS

In another universe, Cassilia inherited the identity of the Mantis. She did not simply inherit the legend—she rebuilt it in her own image.

She redesigned the equipment around speed, flexibility, and close combat. The mask warned her enemies that someone was coming. The blade and Power Fist explained what happened next.
