# Cassilia Story Archive — Website + HyperFrames Storyboard

## Experience Goal
Turn Cassilia from a recurring MOD character into a recognizable original protagonist. Visitors should understand the complete story order, remember the Temerius/Unity twist, and naturally continue into the connected MOD pages.

## Website Beats

### Beat 01 — Who Is Cassilia?
- Full-screen character hero.
- Slow image push-in and orbit-line rotation.
- Headline: `WHO IS CASSILIA?`
- CTA: `BEGIN HER STORY`.

### Beat 02 — Countless Lives
- Editorial profile layout.
- Role labels transition through Vanguard, Explorer, Ranger, Pirate, Mantis, Starborn.
- Mood shifts from human history to Starborn isolation.

### Beat 03 — The Stolen Relic
- THE TERMINUS cover and sword silhouette dominate the frame.
- Faint Temerius whispers appear in the margins.
- Reveal Kadovar, the Oathbreaker.
- End beat: `THE SWORD HAD FOUND A NEW HOST.`

### Beat 04 — First Encounter
- In-game temple screenshots.
- Copy establishes the Lodge dataslate and the battle.
- Moving warning band: `THE PLAYER DEFEATS CASSILIA`.

### Beat 05 — She Did Not Die
- Near-black transition.
- Unity rings assemble and brighten.
- Cassilia is sent to another universe at the instant of defeat.
- This is the central story correction and must be unmistakable.

### Beat 06 — Cassilia's Soul
- Split-screen universes.
- A pulsing bridge forms at the center.
- Explain that a living Cassilia is temporarily pulled into the player's universe.

### Beat 07 — Lives Across Universes
- Old Constellation Uniform: home and belonging.
- Power Fist: humor, strength, direct combat.
- Mantis: inherited legend rebuilt in her own image.
- Casual Outfit: human moments between battles.

### Beat 08 — Who Owns Temerius?
- Minimal black/gold composition.
- Kadovar → Cassilia → Player.
- Answer: none of them may truly own it.

### Beat 09 — Connected MOD Archive
- Six MOD cards with direct Nexus links.
- Use story order rather than release order.

### Beat 10 — The Story Has Only Just Begun
- Return to the sword and Unity imagery.
- Final lines: Cassilia alive; Kadovar still out there; Temerius in the player's hands.
- End CTA to explore MODs.
