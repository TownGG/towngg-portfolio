# Cassilia Story Archive — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
A cinematic Starfield character archive with the dramatic pacing of a premium game campaign website. The page should feel like a recovered Starborn dossier: dark, elegant, mysterious, tactile, and story-first. Large editorial typography, edge-to-edge game imagery, restrained gold accents, deep shadows, Unity rings, soul-link lines, and slow parallax create a premium experience. Motion should feel heavy and deliberate rather than flashy.

## Colors
- `#05070B` — primary void background
- `#ECE6D9` — warm archival white / primary text
- `#CBA25B` — ancient relic gold / active accents
- `#F2D18D` — luminous gold / Unity highlights
- `#8CCBE0` — cold Starborn blue / alternate-universe light
- `#77B698` — Mantis green / equipment chapter accent

## Typography
- Display / narrative: `Cormorant Garamond`, 500–700
- Interface / labels: `Rajdhani`, 500–700
- Body / navigation: `Inter`, 300–800

## Image Direction
- Use real in-game screenshots for story beats.
- Use MOD cover images as chapter dividers and archive cards.
- Apply restrained saturation, strong contrast, and soft vignettes.
- Keep Cassilia's face and equipment readable; never bury the subject under effects.
- Prefer large crops with intentional focal points instead of small card mosaics.

## Motion Rules
- Scroll-driven motion should be slow, cinematic, and reversible.
- Parallax range should stay within 6–12% to avoid nausea.
- Text entrances use 40–60px vertical travel with soft ease-out.
- Unity rings rotate slowly and scale subtly.
- Temerius whispers should emerge faintly, never become a noisy glitch effect.
- Mobile should simplify layered motion and preserve story readability.

## What NOT to Do
- No neon rainbow gradients.
- No generic cyberpunk HUD overload.
- No rapid camera shakes or constant looping animation.
- No rounded SaaS-style cards as the dominant visual language.
- No low-contrast body copy on busy imagery.
- No effects that imply Cassilia died; she entered the Unity and remains alive.
